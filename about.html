Araya Films is an independent creative studio driven by a passion for visual storytelling and a belief in the power of film to connect people, evoke emotion, and leave a lasting impression.

With a background in film and filmmaking experience spanning nearly a decade, our work moves across commercials, documentaries, events, real estate, and narrative films. From collaborating with established brands to capturing real people and meaningful moments, we approach every project with a distinct perspective, a commitment to craft, and an eye for the stories worth telling.

We believe every story has something at its heart an emotion, an idea, a person, or a moment waiting to be seen. Our approach is to find that essence and bring it to life through thoughtful direction, intentional cinematography, and carefully crafted images. Whether creating a film for a brand or documenting life as it unfolds, we aim to make work that feels honest, looks distinctive, and stays with the viewer long after the screen fades to black.

At Araya Films, it has never been just about making images. It is about finding meaning in them.
